ABOUT PLURGASM

Peace · Love · Unity · Respect

PLURGASM is a free guide to US raves and electronic music festivals. We put the festival calendar, lineups, rave brands, scene news and safety info in one place, so you can spend less time digging through ten tabs and more time planning your next weekend.

What You'll Find Here

Rave Schedule Every major US festival by month, with dates marked as confirmed, expected or TBA. Festival Guides Lineups, locations and know-before-you-go info for the big events. Rave Brands Outfits, flow toys, hydration packs and gear, sorted by category. Blog Festival news, lineup breakdowns, genre guides and rave culture. Safety Harm reduction basics and the organizations doing the work on the ground. Rave History How the scene got here, from warehouse parties to stadium festivals.

Who's Behind It

PLURGASM is made by ravers, for ravers. It's run by the creator behind @BeastModeBass, an EDM and bass music meme and content page. We go to these shows, we follow these artists, and we built the site we wished existed when we were planning our own festival seasons.

PLURGASM is independent. We aren't owned by or affiliated with any festival, promoter or ticketing company.

How We Cover Things

  • Sources first. News and factual posts link to official announcements and credible outlets in a Sources list at the end.
  • Honest dates. On the schedule, a date is only marked confirmed once the festival announces it. Everything else is labeled expected or TBA.
  • Original images. Our cover images are made in-house. We don't use official festival or artist artwork.
  • Safety info isn't medical advice. Our harm reduction content points you to established organizations. For anything medical, talk to a professional, and at a festival, find the medical tent.
  • Corrections. Spot something wrong or out of date? Email us and we'll fix it.

How PLURGASM Is Funded

The site is free to use. Some brand links are referral links or come with discount codes, which can earn us a small commission at no extra cost to you. Brands and festivals can also join our partner program for featured placement. Paid placement never changes festival dates, lineups or our safety information.
