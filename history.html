History of Rave

Dance music didn't arrive fully formed. It got built — in warehouses and basements, on sound systems hauled into fields, by people who kept showing up. This is our running chronology of the moments that got us here: the breakthroughs, the festivals, the hardware, the losses, and the small footnotes that turned out to matter.

Tap any marker to open it. Bigger markers mean bigger moments, and the colour tells you what kind of moment it was. On desktop you can flip the whole thing sideways.
