Festival Info · The Essentials

KNOW BEFORE YOU GO

Breakaway Mass is an 18+ city festival — no camping, no overnight, just two evenings of music inside and outside the Palladium. It's cashless, re-entry isn't a thing, and the bag rules are strict, so here's the short version of what to expect.

LOOK OUT FOR EACH OTHER

End Overdose is an official on-site partner at this festival, providing naloxone (Narcan) and teaching people how to recognize and respond to an overdose. Stop by their booth — it takes a few minutes to learn, and it can save a life on the worst night of someone else's. To go deeper, check out our guide to rave harm-reduction organizations.

Dates, Hours & Entry
  • Friday Aug 21 and Saturday Aug 22, 2026, with the festival running roughly 4 PM to 11 PM each day.
  • 18+ to enter with a valid government-issued photo ID; some ticket types are 21+.
  • The event is fully cashless — bring a card or your phone.
  • No re-entry once you leave for the day, so plan accordingly.
Venue
  • The Palladium, 261 Main St, Worcester, MA — the festival uses both the indoor venue and the outdoor grounds.
  • About an hour from Boston, so plan your ride home ahead of time — the show ends around 11 PM.
Tickets
  • Tickets are delivered through Universe roughly two weeks before the festival — don't panic if yours isn't in your inbox yet.
  • All sales are final; Breakaway holds a strict no-refund policy.
Bags & Bottles
  • Bags must be 14″×6″×14″ or smaller — backpacks aren't allowed.
  • One empty clear reusable water bottle (1L or less) per person is fine; refill inside.
  • Hydration packs are allowed if they come in empty and have no more than two pockets.
Good Things to Bring
  • Earplugs — your future self will thank you.
  • LED hoops, whips, gloves, and other flow toys are welcome.
  • Non-aerosol sunscreen and a poncho if the sky looks moody.
  • Wearable glow items (16 oz or less) and small misting fans are fine.
  • Prescription meds are OK in their original labeled bottles.
Leave at Home
  • No outside food or drink, and no glass or aerosols of any kind.
  • No totems or poles, no chairs, and no umbrellas.
  • No professional cameras or GoPros, and no drones.
  • No illegal substances — including marijuana — and no individual glowsticks.
Weather
  • The show goes on rain or shine.
  • Severe weather can pause the event; refunds aren't issued for weather delays.

This is a summary — check the official Breakaway Mass page for full, current details.
