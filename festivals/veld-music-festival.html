The Festival

WHAT IS VELD?

VELD Music Festival is one of Canada's biggest electronic music festivals, held each summer at Downsview Park in Toronto, Ontario. It is a three-day, 18+ festival that draws huge crowds for mainstage dance music, and it is widely regarded as the second-biggest EDM festival in the country.

The focus is EDM, with house and bass woven in — big-room headliners, packed mainstage sets and a city-festival atmosphere, with downtown Toronto a short trip away. Lineups are announced ahead of each edition, so check back for the 2027 reveal.
