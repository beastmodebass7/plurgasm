Festival Info · The Essentials

KNOW BEFORE YOU GO

Ubbi Dubbi is a two-day, 18+ outdoor festival in the middle of Fort Worth. There's no camping and no re-entry, and the bag rules are strict. Here's the short version.

LOOK OUT FOR EACH OTHER

Texas in late April can be hot. The festival has free water stations, so bring an empty reusable bottle, use the buddy system and know where the medical tents are. Our guide to rave harm-reduction organizations is worth a read before you go.

Dates, Tickets & Entry
  • Friday and Saturday, April 23–24, 2027.
  • Early bird GA started at $159, with payment plan deposits as low as $29. Prices go up by tier, so check the official site for what's left.
  • 2027 hours haven't been posted yet. In 2025, gates opened at 3 PM, with parking and box office opening at 2 PM.
  • 18+ with a valid government photo ID. 21+ to buy or drink alcohol. Expired IDs, school IDs and copies are not accepted, and security may ask for a second ID.
  • No re-entry. Once you leave, you're out for the day.
  • Expect a full TSA-style search at the gate.
Bags & What to Bring
  • Allowed: small single-compartment bags and purses up to 12" x 12", and fanny packs up to 10" x 9".
  • Hydration packs are allowed only if the bladder is clear and removable for inspection.
  • Not allowed: backpacks, multi-pocket bags, outside food or drinks (one sealed single-serve water bottle is OK), glass, aerosol sunscreen, liquid makeup, umbrellas, chairs, tents and pets (service animals excepted).
  • Good to bring: an empty reusable bottle, earplugs, a poncho and factory-sealed travel-size sunscreen.
Venue & Getting There
  • Panther Island Pavilion, on the Trinity River in Fort Worth. It's about 35 miles from downtown Dallas.
  • Fully outdoors, rain or shine, except for dangerous weather. Check the forecast before you go.
  • There is no camping, and camping in the parking lots isn't allowed. Book a hotel or plan a ride home. The official site has a hotel booking link.
  • Parking and rideshare drop-off details are on the official site's location page.
