DUSK TILL DAWN — TWO WEEKENDS

Next: May 14–16 + May 21–23, 2027 Las Vegas Motor Speedway, NV 18+ 2 Weekends · Kinetic Stages 1-weekend from $399 · 2-weekend from $599

The crown jewel of North American raves. For 2027, EDC expands to two weekends for the first time in its history — EDC Dusk (May 14–16) and EDC Dawn (May 21–23), with a 12-day Dusk Till Dawn run bridging them. Lower per-weekend capacity means more room and easier travel, with the same kinetic stages, art cars, and sunrise sets under the electric sky.
