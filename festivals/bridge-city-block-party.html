Festival Info · The Essentials

KNOW BEFORE YOU GO

Bridge City is a compact two-day, 18+ block party on the downtown Portland waterfront — short days, no camping, and no re-entry once you're in. Here's the short version.

LOOK OUT FOR EACH OTHER

Six hours a day is quick, but Portland weather and a bass rail will still take it out of you — hydrate, and stick with your crew. Our guide to rave harm-reduction organizations is worth a read before you go.

Dates, Hours & Entry
  • Saturday and Sunday, September 5–6, 2026.
  • Gates open at 4 PM; the event runs 4 PM–10 PM each day.
  • No re-entry — once you leave for the day, you're out. Plan your food, layers, and phone charge accordingly.
  • 18+ with a government-issued photo ID. 21+ to drink, with a separate ID check for wristbands.
Venue
  • Tom McCall Waterfront Park, downtown Portland, OR — right on the Willamette, walkable from downtown and easy to reach on MAX.
  • Fully outdoors, rain or shine. It's Portland in September, so bring a layer and expect the show to run wet if it wants to.
  • There is no camping — sort out a hotel or a ride home.

This is a summary — always check the official Bridge City Block Party site for full, current details on tickets, entry, and what you can bring.
