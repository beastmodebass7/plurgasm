The Festival

WHAT IS OSHEAGA?

OSHEAGA is Montreal's premier summer music and arts festival, held on the islands of Parc Jean-Drapeau. It is a three-day, all-ages event and one of the largest festivals in Canada.

Unlike a pure dance-music festival, OSHEAGA is multi-genre: headline electronic acts share the bill with indie, hip-hop and pop. That makes it a good fit for ravers who also want a broader festival weekend.

OSHEAGA is typically held in early August. Check the next edition above for the latest on dates and tickets.
