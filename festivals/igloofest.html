The Festival

WHAT IS IGLOOFEST?

IGLOOFEST is an outdoor electronic music festival held every winter at the Old Port of Montreal. It bills itself as the coldest party on earth, and it earns the title: thousands of ravers dance outdoors through the Quebec winter, bundled in snowsuits, boots and wild costumes, with Old Montreal as the backdrop.

The sound centres on house, techno and bass, spread across four consecutive weekends in January and February. Because it is held outdoors in deep winter, dressing for the cold is part of the culture — thermal layers, insulated boots, hats and gloves are the norm, and many fans go all-in with full snowsuits.
